# QB-09 passive local recovery

The operator explicitly chooses passive healing near living sculk, rather than requiring an Ancient Altar. This slice follows accepted crop growth and keeps all mechanics in Quiet Below. An Altar may later offer a separate station interaction; it is not a prerequisite for this benefit.

Established or Extensive ecology heals an injured eligible participant within four blocks of owned, physically rooted sculk with at least 25% vitality. Default pace is one health point (half a heart) per ten eligible seconds. Multiple nearby blocks do not stack. Use the ordinary health API; do not grant/remove Regeneration or replace external potion effects, natural healing, hunger rules or maximum health. Surviving in the area reveals the benefit naturally, without farm/player bindings, timed sessions or GM unlocks.

Only alive survival/adventure participants in the configured dimension qualify. Creative, spectator, author, absent, departed and fully healthy players get no stored healing credit. Pause/operation hold/empty play, unresolved or missing roots, leaving reach, weaker sculk and disabled recovery clear or reset the wait. Re-entry/restart starts a fresh interval. A final native check precedes each heal; no offline catch-up. Each tracked cooldown retains one nearby living source as its proof. At most 256 such proofs and player health/eligibility are checked every tick, preventing an unsampled player from banking credit after a transient exit/full heal. Losing that proof resets the wait conservatively even if another source can subsequently be found. One player per tick gets a nearest-source search or a guarded heal; both paths stay within 513 metadata positions/tick. A fair UUID queue handles roster changes. Larger groups can wait longer than the selected minimum interval. No new entities/block entities or world scans.

Game setup gains **Local healing**, with enable/disable, faster/slower/reset and current state/range/pace. Rates are 2/5/10/20/40 seconds. Rules are campaign-journal state and must survive restart and maintenance checkpoints. Core's existing permissions/menu/participant/time services remain generic and unchanged. Changes in rates reset derived cooldowns.

Verification: pure timing/entry/exit/cut/weak/pause/range/source-stacking/health/capacity bounds; strict rule replay; checkpoint round trip preserving disabled and nondefault recovery tuning; actual native player health increase near a living Established network, controls, distant/healthy/creative/nonparticipant/pause/cut negative checks, restart and an authentic playable review. Native fixtures isolate natural regeneration only for automated assertions, then restore it for human review. No Hunger/Darkness cost is silently coupled to healing in this slice; those proposals remain later balance work.

A finite full-city index/unload/restart/20TPS trial and stopped-world journal maintenance pass; prolonged operations remain open. These later changes do not retroactively add costs to the original benefit-only review build.
