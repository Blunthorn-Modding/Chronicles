# Deferred tools: implementation and verification

1 November 2026, dev65, branch `feat/core-operations-and-scenario-authoring`. This delivers the combined tools for organizer review; map integration, final content and balance remain separate gates.

## Delivered

- Core archives terminal Event/source evidence before reclaiming bounded live capacity. Pending and uncertain operations remain protected, and exact archived retries cannot replay effects. The initial policy is an explicit non-active operator action; automatic quiet-period scheduling remains a follow-up.
- Core exposes real changed role assignments and completed first-entry operations as Event sources. No-op role choices and deferred kits do not report completion.
- Core owns encounter compositions, world bindings, native authoring and Event spawning. A single atomic catalog publishes composition and binding changes. Deterministic geometry errors are refused before execution; every loaded entity footprint and candidate is preflighted before claims. Interrupted outcomes retain exact UUID/stage evidence and require explicit review.
- Core exports reusable roles, books, loot, encounter compositions and disabled Event templates as a build input. Quiet Below loads an optional reviewed packaged resource. Map geometry, role destinations, assignments, discoveries and progress stay outside that export. The authoritative role YAML now travels in the world, with guarded read-only legacy bootstrap.
- Quiet Below supplies four additional mysterious Altar prayers. Core owns their durable claims, timers, recovery and temporary modifiers. Repair/sight costs survive death and reconnect until occupied active-time expiry. Eradication removes ongoing effects without undoing completed repairs, healing or consumed offerings.

## Corrections and limits

Preserved failed runs exposed a duplicate blur call, a capped-choice completion projection error and compact creation controls incorrectly left in the detail pane. All were corrected and rerun. Test fixtures also needed grounded drop/spawn platforms, normal difficulty after restart, valid username length, actual widget typing/click sequences and an explicit resume before reopening a workstation. Diagnostics showed the reconnect refusal was the accepted paused-container policy; a speculative block-entity lookup change was reverted.

These are focused native proofs, not a new three-hour soak or a maximum-entity combat benchmark. Vanilla AI, later deaths/despawns, human audibility and final map/group balance remain outside those assertions. Original soak evidence and the previous review world are retained. No main merge or Modrinth publication is part of this delivery.
