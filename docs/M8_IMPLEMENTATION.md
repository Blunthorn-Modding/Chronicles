# M8: shared participation, active time and observations

19 October 2026. Continue after the M7 player-policy slice. Entity encounter groups remain an independent M7 follow-up, not a prerequisite for M8 or Quiet Below ecology. This milestone delivers Core services and operator tools, not a story timetable or ecological simulation.

## Visible behavior

Players explicitly join participation with `/chronicle join`, or a non-active author enrolls them through **Session > Participants**. Default membership is empty. `/chronicle leave` removes participation and automatic spawn policies privately, without moving the player or deleting delivery/arrival history. Spectators, offline players and author walks do not count. Participation is independent of public dossiers and secret campaign roles.

The shared clock advances by simulation ticks only while the session is ACTIVE, a participant is eligible online and no durable operation hold exists. It pauses without catch-up during pause, inactivity, empty play, holds and downtime. Save on phase/occupancy changes, before delivery and every 60 seconds; an abrupt crash may lose up to a minute of uncheckpointed elapsed time, delaying a timer. Existing reviewed M7 loot-cycle timing remains independent in this slice; new observations and later ecology use this shared service.

Campaigns register bounded observation definitions: ID, label, public text, optional vanilla sound and audience (all eligible participants or eligible participants in the bound region). Core contains no Quiet Below text. There are no default bindings. An operator explicitly saves/replaces a manual, region-entry, active-time delay or flag-condition binding in **Session > Observations**, choosing a saved region and optional flag prerequisite. Timers use seconds of active occupied play since arming. Entry means first eligible presence in the bound region, including a participant already inside when armed, as proposed in Quiet Below's design. Simultaneous presence produces one group delivery. Late joiners receive no replay after completion.

Manual preview/fire and automatic conditions share one completion ID per observation. Preview is read-only; manual fire requires ACTIVE, eligible recipients, satisfied prerequisites and explicit confirmation. Recheck the exact binding/definition/session/audience and a 30-second preview expiry. A manual override may fire a timer early but cannot bypass participation, region audience or flag requirements. Resetting completion is an explicit non-active author action. Turning a binding off retains its history. Replacing a binding does not implicitly clear completion.

## Boundaries and persistence

Definitions/catalog, membership, clock and cue binding/delivery are Core-owned. Quiet Below registers optional city/distant cues only; these do not create sculk, unlock benefits or set story flags. Sound is sent privately to the selected recipients at their own position; this slice does not claim directional mine acoustics. Delivery never uses a public world sound broadcast. Existing flag-event callbacks remain pure and unchanged.

New independent `<world>/chronicle/play-runtime.json` schema 1 stores revision, checkpointed active ticks, enrolled UUIDs, revision-pinned bindings and delivery claims. Store strict bounded JSON, duplicate/unknown/future rejection, atomic durable replacement, previous backup and outside-edit detection. No migration or rewriting of other state. Definition fingerprints and region revisions reject stale bindings. One claim is saved before sending text/sound; then DELIVERED is recorded. A crash/failure can leave CLAIMED uncertainty and never automatically replays; operator reset is deliberate. These are delivery attempts, not an exactly-once audio guarantee or a world-mutation journal. Failures disable this service and preserve evidence while other Core tools remain available.

Bound membership to 512, bindings to 128, saved claims to 512 and catalog to 128. Evaluate configured bounded conditions without terrain scans or chunk loading; emit at most one automatic observation per tick. No audience means no claim. Preview/fire data and membership are operator-private; recipients receive only their own chosen public cue. Server derives all positions/eligibility; GUI mutations keep finite actions, permission checks and stale-frame tokens. Background clock-only refreshes must not recreate controls.

## Operator workflow

Import `../client/Chronicle-M8-1.21.10.mrpack`. Open O, then **Session > Participants**. In non-active author mode select a player and confirm **Enroll** or **Exclude**. Players can use `/chronicle join` and `/chronicle leave` themselves during any phase. Exclude removes participation only; self-leave also removes the player's automatic spawn policies.

Under **Session > Observations**, select a registered cue. Choose a saved region if needed, an optional existing flag prerequisite, and timer seconds in the text field. **Manual**, **Entry**, **Timer** or **Condition** saves that trigger; replacing an existing binding requires a second confirmation. Condition requires a flag; entry and region-audience cues require a region. Existing flags are available in the picker; advanced `/chronicle flag set` creates additional flags. The two campaign examples are public placeholder cues, not facts about the ecology.

During ACTIVE play, select an armed observation, **Preview**, then **Fire > Confirm: Fire**. Preview reports the exact eligible recipients and changes no history. Moving players into/out of the region, participation changes, session changes or expiration require a fresh preview. Automatic triggers may complete between preview and confirmation; the once-only claim refuses a duplicate. **Off** removes the trigger, **Reset** deliberately clears completion in non-active author mode. CLAIMED means a delivery attempt was interrupted; it is never automatically retried.

The clock and eligible count appear on Session, Participants and Observations screens. No participants or bindings are installed by default. `play-runtime.json` is internal durable state, not a live-edit configuration file; use the screen while the service is running. Stale region/definition references refuse delivery until explicitly replaced. A persistence failure disables this optional runtime for the current process; stop and inspect preserved data/logs before restarting. Optional sound is private and nondirectional; audible quality and balance require the licensed-client review.
