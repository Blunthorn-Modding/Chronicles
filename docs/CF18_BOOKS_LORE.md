## Two-route revision,25 October

The organizer replaces the original table-only distribution design with two automatic routes: one optional specific lore book plus a percentage on each existing bound chest, and a distinct Lore books section within ordinary loot tables. Both share the book's central spawning rules. Add an operator control to keep a particular book available in a loot-table group until found during ACTIVE play. This revision remains a concrete workflow mockup for review, not an installed feature.

## Confirmed product rules

- Books are named definitions: stable ID, title, author, organizer description, ordered plain-text pages and distribution rule. Core owns this generic feature. Campaign mods register defaults; organizers can edit them or create custom books in Advanced > Books & Lore. Quiet Below supplies the five journals and their prose.
- A real player taking a managed book into their inventory counts as finding it. The result is campaign-wide, not per player or session. Normal ACTIVE gameplay counts; map-building, author previews and editor activity do not. Looking into a chest, holding its item only on the cursor, generating it, or reading a preview does not count.
- Rules: **Distribution off** (no new copies), **Spawn once** (at most one managed issuance, whether or not it is found), **Until found** (continue eligible rolls until the first inventory discovery), and **Repeat** (ordinary repeated rolls). Recipe journals default to Until found. Changing a rule affects future eligibility, not existing copies or discovery history.
- Books & Lore owns content and the one central rule for each book. Each existing bound chest may additionally select one specific book and a0-100% chance. Normal Loot Tables retain the complete existing item add/select/edit/remove, chance and min/max quantity controls under **Items**, and add a distinct **Lore books** section; the latter references named books and their independent chances without copying pages, author or spawning rules. Chests remains the sole physical registry and its existing refill schedule is reused. Books & Lore shows separate read-only Direct chests and Loot tables usage links.
- A direct chest setting takes precedence over the same book's table entry on that chest: one effective chance and at most one automatic copy per fill, not two rolls. Other table items and books remain eligible. Clearing the setting restores the table chance on future fills. Saving either route changes no physical contents immediately and resets no completed fill or global book history.
- Entering ACTIVE play clears setup Editing, matching the current Core lifecycle. Definition, table and per-chest configuration stay locked throughout ACTIVE play; the operator keep-placed control does not require Editing.
- Operators can **Keep placed until found** during ACTIVE play without Editing. Select one existing loot table, not a fixed chest. One request per book keeps a managed copy available across that table's bound chests, bypassing chance. Try a book-only insertion immediately without refilling other loot or resetting timers; full/unloaded/uncertain targets remain pending. Existing scheduled fills reconcile the request and may move its copy within the table group. Normal inventory receipt completes it campaign-wide. Pause, session end and restart do not reset it; an operator may explicitly Stop keeping without deleting existing books/history.
- An unfound book with Distribution off or a previously issued Spawn once rule requires explicit **Override spawning rule for this request only**. This changes neither the central rule nor its history. A found book cannot start another keep request or override discovery. A request's own issuance does not block its continued maintenance under Spawn once. The ordinary two chance-based routes retain their central rules.
- Retain authentic managed lore copies already waiting in a bound chest through normal refills until they are taken. Finding a volume elsewhere stops eligible Until found rolls; it does not erase copies waiting elsewhere or a later maintained copy. Ordinary items and unmanaged written books retain full-replacement behavior. No force-loaded chunks, structure scans, new inventory scheduler, quest unlocks or session quotas.
- Keep the accepted journal prose unchanged during this work. Later, after core functionality, review it together with events and the rest of the campaign narrative. The handcrafted map remains pending.

## Boundary and data flow

`Campaign book defaults -> Core book catalog/config -> direct bound-chest book OR Loot Table/Lore books reference -> merge by book ID and apply central rules -> existing fill transaction -> vanilla written-book item -> authoritative inventory discovery receipt`.

The direct setting belongs to the existing binding as optional book ID/chance metadata, with its existing target identity, revision and normal table reference. Do not create another chest registry. Preserve existing cycle receipts when changing that metadata, just as retyping currently avoids a fresh immediate refill. An absent direct setting retains old behavior. Any future schema extension must retain existing bindings, ordinary entries and pending recovery operations. Named references/chances are the only data at either distribution source; spawning/discovery settings appear only in Books & Lore.

Definitions belong in `config/chronicle/books.json`, schema1. Existing arbitrary written-book components remain supported ordinary loot and are not silently claimed or converted. Registered defaults merge under complete organizer overrides without rewriting existing files, as with current loot types. Source ownership is catalog metadata; it is not an invented author or player-facing lore. Default IDs remain stable when titles change. Custom creation generates an ID server-side so the UI requires no ID typing. Registered defaults can be disabled; referenced definitions cannot be deleted blindly. Existing physical books keep their original text after edits. Legacy raw items and author previews are not retroactively turned into managed discoveries. Every newly managed named book carries valid provenance; Repeat ignores recorded discovery for future spawn eligibility rather than erasing or resetting it.

Bound plaintext limits:256 current definitions,32 title characters,80 author characters (blank allowed),256 description characters,64 pages,1024 characters per page and16384 page characters per definition. A new empty definition stays Distribution off until at least one nonblank page exists. Preserve deliberate blank pages and their order. Reject duplicate IDs, unknown fields, malformed/future schemas, oversized files and non-finite values. Use strict bounded JSON and a valid backup plus forced atomic replacement. Publish new in-memory state only after persistence succeeds; optimistic saves compare the expected definition and observed file bytes. Page text becomes literal vanilla Text, never a command-bearing JSON component supplied by a client.

The UI uses bounded typed Core menu forms, private operator responses and stale-token/expected-definition validation. Add the page/action payloads with a clear matching-client protocol version. No read-only player screen receives unpublished book definitions. Definition/table/direct-setting mutations require permission-level2 operators with non-active author editing. The separately confirmed keep-placed action also requires operator authority but is available during ACTIVE play without requiring Editing; a runtime hold or stale request still blocks mutation. Safe pending requests do not force a target load. Browse/preview is read-only; preview uses a local ordinary BookScreen and never inserts a server item or discovery tag. Existing popup/widgets, Clean/Debug and compact navigation remain the presentation framework. Native preview shows the actual wrapping; editor warns if the chosen font cannot fit the page.

## Issuance/discovery transaction plan

Reuse the existing `ChestContentService.begin/complete/abortUnchanged` durable transaction and `ChestContentStore` rather than introducing a second independently committed issuance file. Extend its persisted state with a bounded generic lore-delivery ledger and a campaign namespace UUID. Extend pending operations with explicit managed-book delivery claims. Read existing chest schemas without resetting pending work; write the new schema only on an actual mutation. Every existing state-copy path must preserve the new ledger.

For each book, retain a server-generated delivery token, whether an issuance completed, outstanding pending operation IDs and the first discovery receipt. A token is tied to that campaign/book, not to mutable title text. Copies may share the same valid token, keeping discovery history bounded instead of growing one receipt per refill. Repeat remains eligible even after discovery; it does not require a parallel untracked book type. Managed stacks carry campaign/book/token provenance in server-owned custom data; validation consults the durable ledger. Plain vanilla or author-preview copies have no valid managed receipt and cannot complete discovery.

On a successful managed roll, reserve its claim **in the same durable save** that publishes the pending chest intent, before changing inventory. A once-only rule is blocked by either a completed issuance or a still-pending reservation. A batch-local reservation set prevents two candidate chests in the same existing four-target batch from preparing the same once-only book. Completion after Minecraft's confirmed flush finalizes issuance. `abortUnchanged`, used only after proof of the original target inventory, releases an unmaterialized reservation without resetting an earlier completed issuance. Existing exact before/after recovery remains authoritative; no second recovery queue or invisible retry.

Normal refills preserve valid managed stacks already waiting in their current slots, except that an active keep-placed request may use the safely reconciled same-table relocation described below. Refill protection prevents loss; it does not permanently pin that maintained copy to one chest. Merge direct/table candidates by book ID before rolling: the direct chance replaces a matching table chance, including a deliberate0%; different table books still roll. A preserved copy already waiting suppresses another automatic copy of the same book in that chest. Allocate successful lore rolls into remaining capacity before ordinary item rolls, so normal material rolls cannot silently displace a configured clue. A direct roll has priority if space cannot fit all passed lore candidates; claim only copies actually planned for placement. Limited space must not cause a global hold or consume a Spawn once claim for an unplaced book. Prevent duplicate named references within one table; references in different tables are legitimate. Unknown references block a new save/reload with an actionable error, never degrade into anonymous generic books. Distribution off is intentional ineligibility, not a broken reference.

Observe completed server-side inventory transfers and ordinary pickup insertion, not client claims or book-opening packets. A cursor-only/full-inventory/failed transfer is not a discovery. Only a valid issued campaign/book/token in an actual player's inventory can create the first idempotent receipt. Check existing held inventory on reconnect as a recovery path. A persistence failure keeps the authoritative prior state and uses the existing operation hold/error surface; do not suppress future copies from an uncommitted receipt. Reading, dropping, copying or losing an already discovered book does not reverse that historical discovery. No history-reset UI is promised in the first slice.

## Keep placed until found

This replaces the former one-off Place now flow. All three shortcuts open one table-scoped request: from a book choose an existing table; from a table Lore books row use that table; from a chest choose a book using that chest's current table. The confirmation explicitly says the copy may move between chests using that table. It bypasses chance, but does not trigger a full loot refill or change its clock. A request may name a registered valid book absent from the table's ordinary lore rolls; it does not add a permanent table entry or direct chest setting.

Show the current table and available/waiting status beside the selected book. Manage kept placement opens the existing request, never creates a second one; Stop keeping cancels future maintenance and leaves actual books and discovery history alone. Changing table requires stopping the old request and explicitly creating a new one. Complete it on the first normal campaign-wide player-inventory receipt. Completion stops maintenance but retains delivery provenance and last-known copy records until authoritative inventory reconciliation proves their disposition; finding another copy must not lose protection for one still waiting. Found volumes cannot restart it. No per-session reset, discovery reset, or post-found override.

Persist a bounded request keyed by campaign/book alongside the existing shared delivery ledger, including table ID, pinned revisions, request-scoped rule override, maintained-copy provenance/location or unresolved state, and completion/cancellation audit. Pin the central rule revision/effective decision. If it changes during pause/reload, retain the request and last-known copy but suspend new placement/relocation with Review updated rule. The operator must explicitly Confirm updated rule (with a new request-specific override when required) or Stop keeping. Own issuance under an unchanged approved rule does not count as a rule revision. Recheck external completed/pending Spawn once reservations too: if another route claims the book while this request is deferred, suspend until explicitly overridden; an issued boolean alone cannot distinguish this request's own provenance from that external claim. Source and target identities must be checked against the current binding/table association. Changes/reload/unbind must not orphan tracking or cause a blind new copy. If the source no longer belongs to the table, reconcile safely once its actual inventory is known; do not duplicate it at another location. Missing/no bound table targets remain pending with an actionable status.

On activation, adopt a valid waiting managed copy in the selected group if one is already known, otherwise try a book-only insertion through the existing inventory transaction engine. No ordinary items are overwritten. An unresolved/unloaded known source prevents speculative replacement or relocation. Full/unloaded/uncertain target results do not consume a fresh issuance. No forced loads or independent retry scheduler: later normal existing fill opportunities reconcile pending requests, including session-start fills after resuming. An explicit operator retry may use that same book-only path, if implemented; it is not required in the first UI slice.

At a scheduled refill, preserve availability rather than pinning the original chest. A successful plan may retain the copy or relocate it to another eligible bound chest of the SAME table. Reconcile one request across the entire table/cycle rather than rolling it independently for each chest or each four-target batch. Suppress another ordinary/direct chance issuance for the maintained book during that plan; do not erase legitimate pre-existing copies from other sources. Other ordinary items/books keep their existing chance and replacement behavior. Cancelling the request returns future eligibility to the central rules without deleting a waiting copy.

Cross-chest movement needs a shared recoverable transfer intent with exact source/destination before/after inventories, not two unrelated insertion/deletion transactions. CF18c must resolve this extension to the existing content-operation engine before implementation: durable reservation, same-server-tick inventory mutation, proven flush/completion and bounded exact recovery must prevent a duplicate, lost clue, or repeat-click issue at every interruption. If either inventory cannot be established, retain the known source and defer the move. Requests survive restart without replaying a completed transfer or resetting Spawn once/discovery. This is architecture work and acceptance criteria, not native proof supplied by the mockup.
