# M9b: vitality, recovery and extent tuning

The operator accepts organic growth and authorizes further implementation. Disconnected sculk **loses vitality but keeps its dormant blocks**, as explicitly confirmed. Physical reconnection permits recovery even from zero; there is no deadline, terrain decay, independent root or catalyst upkeep. This slice remains Quiet Below-owned; Core supplies its existing generic tool UI and occupied-play eligibility only.

Each owned position has lazy, fixed-point vitality. New/legacy cells begin at 100%. Detached or completely sealed cells lose vitality; rooted cells exposed to air recover, twice as quickly in bright exposure (>7 light). No oxygen/airflow simulation. A 25% vitality threshold permits local growth/native responses; a severed physical connection disables these immediately regardless of remaining vitality. Vitality does not obstruct the physical joining of placed sculk or reconnection of dormant branches. Defaults for review: full loss in 10 eligible minutes, full dark recovery in 5 minutes (bright 2.5). These are configurable technical defaults, not settled balance.

Clock: only enabled, occupied ACTIVE ecology with a resolved Heart/network. Unknown/unloaded cells cannot cause weakness. Scan <=128 owned positions and six loaded-only neighbors per tick. Publish mode changes in bounded journal batches. Persist a shared clock and integrated loss/recovery budgets, not a rewritten health value per cell per tick. Ten-second checkpoints, plus pause/hold/shutdown flushes, retain spent eligible ticks without downtime catch-up; an abrupt crash may discard less than one checkpoint. Rate changes advance the old budgets before switching, so they never apply retroactively. Removed ownership also removes its health entry. Existing journal history/baselines stay intact; new VITALITY/VITALITY_RULES types require the new build.

Complete bounded assessments report rooted/detached vitality, feeding/sealed/unknown positions and ready rooted count. Extent uses ready rooted count with 12.5% exit hysteresis: default enter Established 256 / Extensive 2048. Unknown assessment retains the previous extent and cannot authorize newly generated advanced features. Changes require indexed, non-active operator editing, and are saved per world. Keep existing speed controls independent.

Verification: pure replay/transition/rate-change/capacity/unknown/pause/reconnection tests; maximum-city metadata/world-read soak; real Minecraft loss/recovery and native hooks; a separate faithful vanilla four-warning Warden summon/dig/despawn rehearsal where feasible. Do not substitute direct entity spawning or warning/cooldown injection. Keep production ecology disabled. Preserve the authenticated review map and saved settings via stopped backup/in-place upgrade, leave paused, update client pack with its accepted defaults, then request review of vitality timing/feedback. M10 benefits, M11 stations and M12 Heart destruction remain subsequent slices. No milestone/session narrative schedule is introduced.

Operational limit: the existing append log still holds safely at 64 MiB or 150,000 records; compaction is not implemented. The maximum-source and exposure-churn tests establish bounded per-tick work, not an indefinite production soak. Large-city native chunk/load-order tests, compaction and human balance review remain M9 follow-up before prolonged production activation.
