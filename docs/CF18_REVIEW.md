# CF18 combined review (dev52)

Use the updated [client pack](../../client/Chronicle-M9-1.21.10.mrpack) and the preset Current review server, `127.0.0.1:25589`. The matching Core menu protocol is v2. The journal fixture is a disposable review map, separate from the unfinished handcrafted map.

At spawn, chests at **0 64 0** and **4 64 0** use the same Research journals table. It has normal bread/iron loot, two named volumes with independent25% chances, and a30-active-second refill interval for this review. The chest at **8 64 0** uses Settlement supplies and a direct100% setting for Field journal III. Volumes IV/V can be kept in a chosen table group without adding them to its normal chances. New campaign defaults outside this review retain their original4% journal chances and no timed refill.

1. While play is ended/paused, enable Editing and open Advanced > Books & Lore. Edit metadata/pages, add a blank page, preview and cancel/save. Create a custom book, add a page and choose its distribution rule. Existing physical copies should retain their old text.
2. In Loot Tables, check that Items still supports adding/removing ordinary items and counts. Switch to Lore books, add/remove named volumes and edit percentages. Refill interval and Save apply to the whole table. In Chests, choose a bound chest and its Specific lore book setting; direct settings for the same volume override its table percentage.
3. Stop Editing and start play. Choose Keep placed until found from a book, a table row or a bound chest. It should add/adopt only that book, preserving other contents. Wait for refills: one maintained copy can move between the two shared chests. Stop keeping should leave the actual book alone. Previewing never issues a book.
4. **Switch your player to Survival for discovery testing** (`/gamemode survival`). Creative/editor receipt deliberately does not count. Taking a managed volume into inventory marks it found campaign-wide and completes keeping. Cursor-only handling does not count. A found book cannot restart keeping. The original untracked journals left in the old review chest demonstrate legacy compatibility and do not count as named lore.
5. Check Clean/Debug, compact layout and validation readability. The regular native book reader controls page wrapping with your font/resource pack. Journal prose stays frozen for the final narrative pass.

Building Mode can be enabled explicitly while play is inactive to access the map; it resets on phase changes/restart. Ordinary gameplay remains frozen outside active play. Do not use Creative to assess discovery behavior. The tests already cover actual scheduled movement, full/cursor-only/fleeting inventory receipt and restart; your review is for the complete workflow and presentation.
