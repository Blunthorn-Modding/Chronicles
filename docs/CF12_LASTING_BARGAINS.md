## Persistence and failure contract

Schema1 `world/chronicle/offerings.json` is authoritative. Each receipt has a unique transaction UUID, player UUID, campaign source, offering ID and immutable health/food/speed terms. Counts are by player/source/offering. Strict fields/types/duplicate rejection; bounded file8MiB,16384 total receipts,128 receipts per player,256 closed sources. Initial creation writes a durable sibling initialization marker before creating the ledger; a missing initialized ledger is an error. Hold/refuse new transactions on corruption, external byte changes, IO or capacity failure; preserve evidence, never create an empty fallback or automatically promote a backup.

Commit the whole ledger by forced temporary-file write, exact previous-byte backup and atomic replacement **before** projecting native stats. Token/expected-revision revalidation and stable transaction UUID prevent duplicate consumption/grants. A server crash after commit but before projection is repaired by deriving the same modifiers on login/restart. Because both price and bonus are stats in this slice, no cross-file item/XP exchange is needed. Purchase counts survive death/reconnect. Core stores no reversible duration for lasting bargains.

Closing a campaign source durably removes all its receipts and retains a tombstone. Both its bonuses and sacrifices disappear from online players and from offline players at next load; no later purchase or stale request may reopen it. The future Heart-ending implementation must persist this closure before declaring its cleanup complete. Unrelated campaign sources/modifiers remain intact.

Native projection uses source-scoped persistent Chronicle modifiers for maximum health/movement and a tracked Core food-capacity attribute (default20). If persistent Chronicle modifiers exist without corresponding ledger authority or a closed-source tombstone, hold rather than silently refund. Never remove foreign modifiers. An invalid ledger prevents reconciliation; retain last persisted modifiers. Reconciliation after death, reconnect, purchase and source closure is idempotent. Same-source terms aggregate; movement percentages add to base speed.
