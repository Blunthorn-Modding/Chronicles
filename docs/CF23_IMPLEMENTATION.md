## Agreed behavior and ownership

Events own When, Requirements, Delay and one What happens action list. Core owns the generic mechanics and durable operation evidence; Quiet Below supplies station/ecology/Heart meaning. Multiple prerequisite flags use All/On. Any required flag turning Off resets the full countdown, including a brief Off/On between dispatch ticks. Pauses, holds, lack of eligible participants and downtime do not spend time. Flags remain campaign-wide saved facts and manual GM overrides. Book found and real gameplay opening are separate sources; opening counts as reading, while editor previews do not.

The dedicated [native UI design](CF23_EVENT_UI_DESIGN.md) was lead-reviewed before implementation. Its fixed four-page predecessor and Regions/Flags action-mode split are superseded.

## Source boundaries

- Campaign schema 2 adds atomic per-execution flag outcome receipts and durable Off-transition versions. Same-operation retry does not overwrite later flag changes or depend on bounded audit retention. Existing schema 1 state reads unchanged until a mutation; receipts cap 256, flag patches32, existing flags256.
- Event definition/configuration format 2 add flag outcomes, multiple requirements and registered system sources. Legacy constructors/format 1 encoding and fingerprints remain exact for old immutable archives. PlayStore 3 reads1/2/3. New native form and menu protocol 10 require a matching dev63 pack.
- `EventProgressStore` independently persists arming/source baselines, latched occurrence identity, Off epochs and countdown start on the occupied active clock. Unknown unlock time of an old gated timer requires an explicit edit/save review. Fresh configuration/rearm records existing source facts as baseline; future occurrences alone can trigger it.
- Event execution applies successful terrain changes, then an idempotent atomic flag patch, then claims media. Silent flag-only Events may have no media recipients. Media/world Event audience guards remain. Deliberate rearm preserves execution archives.
- `EventSources` supplies finite registered selectors and strict source receipts. Actual block callbacks and genuine managed book use are distinct from authoring and refused operations. Durable departure, offering, book, Region and scenario evidence can reconcile after interruption. Source receipts pin prerequisite values at the accepted occurrence. An unrelated target does not consume this Event's tracking capacity; cancelled-attempt retention is bounded.
- A trusted silent zero-delay completion may record its progress after its originating departure ends play. Mixed or delayed new source Events are skipped after ending; new world/media effects do not begin after play ends. Already-started executions retain their recovery rules.
- Uncertain source outcomes require an exact reviewed GM decision, with no operation replay. Recovery uses OP2/non-active play/no unrelated hold and does not require enabling Editing under its own hold. Source I/O/corruption failures remain fail-closed and may require stopped-world evidence repair.

## Using progression

Create the saved facts under Advanced **Flags**, then create an Event. For example, choose **Book opened**, select the journal, and add **Update flag → quiet_below:journal_read → On** under What happens. A second Event can require `quiet_below:journal_read` and another flag, wait a configured number of active seconds, then change one or more Regions and send text/sound. The first Event may have only its flag outcome; flags themselves do not execute actions.

Gameplay sources are future occurrences after saving/rearming. Required flags must be On at a discrete source occurrence; enabling a flag later does not resurrect an earlier refused/gated action. Once a qualifying occurrence is latched, a prerequisite turning Off resets its delay. Successful station operations count; opening a station, cancelling a prayer, scraps and unchanged capped Forge equipment do not count as successful prayer/enchantment completion. Block interaction remains a separate generic source when that is the intended condition.

When an operation was interrupted, Events can show a source recovery review. It pins the exact recorded evidence and expires after 30 seconds. Forge reviews include the actor, both submitted items and expected enchanted output. Confirming happened/not happened records source progress, never repeats a prayer, world change, item transfer or enchantment. Fresh preparation with definitively absent durable authority is cancelled automatically only after the owning store verifies its loaded contents/observed absence and excludes live or durable parent operations. Corrupt or externally changed evidence is preserved and held.

An interrupted book discovery is held for review. Its exact saved first-finder record can reconcile automatically. If that record is missing, confirming discovery requires the original player online with the authentic managed book in their inventory; the server saves player data and records that discovery before completing source progress. A same-titled unmarked book is insufficient. Cancelling changes neither inventory nor issuance, and a later attempt receives a fresh identity and prerequisite snapshot.

## Upgrade and operating limits

Use a stopped-world backup before upgrading and use the matching menu protocol 10 client. Campaign schema 2 and PlayStore 3 retain old schema readers; old immutable Event definitions/configurations keep their format 1 bytes and hashes. New editors save format 2. An old gated timer requires an explicit edit/save; its unknown historical prerequisite-unlock time is not guessed. Existing source histories are baselined when an Event is saved or deliberately rearmed.

The source ledger retains at most 1024 committed/pending receipts and 64 cancelled attempts; atomic flag outcomes retain 256 operation receipts. Capacity exhaustion refuses further work and preserves evidence for stopped-world maintenance. These bounds are safety limits, not automatic historical compaction. This change adds no dependencies or license changes. Final handcrafted-map integration, final journal/event prose and realistic group/voice soak remain separate work.
