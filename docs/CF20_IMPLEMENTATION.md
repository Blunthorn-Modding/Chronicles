# CF20 group departures

Implemented and deployed on `feat/core-roles-areas-departures` for the combined dev55 review. Human acceptance remains pending.

**Advanced → Departures** is separate from Locations and Game setup. Create is above the list. A definition selects an existing exact Block site, a boarding Region, two warning texts, and an optional Area change/Start or End. **Bind looked-at block** creates or reuses one ordinary Core Anchor while retaining the other editor fields. The paged selectors retain missing reference IDs rather than silently clearing them. Used definitions remain immutable history.

Quiet Below supplies the initially unbound **The only ship** definition and its island-specific warnings. Core's new-definition wording is generic. Configure the handcrafted map's helm/boarding Region and ship Area in the editor; no default island coordinates, session timetable, copied sculk route or map generation is introduced.

During occupied ACTIVE play an eligible participant uses the exact control block. Only online, alive, participating, non-author, non-spectator characters physically aboard are included. Offline saved positions are ignored. The first review names the exact passengers. Continue opens a distinct stacked final warning; Back/Escape returns to a fresh first review, and Cancel is initially focused. Server-owned, expiring, connection-bound, single-use tokens pin the roster, membership generation, sites, phase and selected Area hash. The membership generation changes on membership edits rather than unrelated clock checkpoints.

When an Area is linked, all loaded-terrain/player/quota/consumer checks, before/after capture and side-effect preparation finish before the irreversible departure boundary. The shared Area hold prevents interaction during that preparation. Cancelling or an invalidated review releases the uncommitted reservation; immutable preparation evidence may remain as an orphan, never as permission to apply the terrain by itself.

`DepartureService.begin` atomically persists the consumed definition, exact departed roster and operation. Native effects then set those accounts to Spectator and save player data. Each passenger receives an immutable receipt only after rereading their saved `.dat` and confirming Spectator. Receipts permit passengers to reconnect at different times after interruption. Only when every committed passenger is acknowledged can the linked Area apply, and only its actual durable COMPLETE receipt advances the departure.

If all enrolled non-departed accounts were aboard, including consideration of offline accounts left behind, the coordinator ends play and persists a campaign-concluded outcome. This outcome survives restart and blocks start/resume/join/new role entry. Pending committed departures also block membership and play-boundary changes until their pinned obligations finish. Operator metadata authoring remains available after conclusion. Departing does not eradicate the ecology, clear altar bargains, erase inventories or reset book discovery.

Authority is stored in `world/chronicle/departures.json`, with independent saved-player receipts under `departure-player-effects/` and linked Area evidence under `area-changes/`. Interrupted effects hold play and retry the same immutable operation; no normal return, clear-departed or reset-history API is exposed. Role clearing can release a seat without restoring participation. Automatic movement/respawn, role entry and normal participation paths consult departure authority; a gamemode change alone does not re-enrol a departed character.
