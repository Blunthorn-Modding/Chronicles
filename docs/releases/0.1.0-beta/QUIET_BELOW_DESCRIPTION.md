# The Quiet Below

An ancient city lies beneath the surface. Its sculk is alive, and it is spreading.

The Quiet Below is a Minecraft roleplay campaign built on **Chronicle Core**. A Relic Heart feeds a living sculk network that grows along connected surfaces. That growth brings benefits as well as dangers, giving players reasons to investigate it, depend on it, resist it, or disagree about what should happen next.

There are no mandatory factions, personal corruption mechanics or fixed events assigned to particular sessions. Players can coexist with the network, sever its connections, contain its spread, weaken and destroy the Heart, or leave aboard a configured ship. The choices belong to the people in the world.

## Beneath the surface

- **A connected sculk ecology:** Growth follows physical connections from the Heart, with configurable fixed or population-based growth rates.
- **A visibly living network:** Healthy, fading and dormant sculk, sensors, catalysts and shriekers reflect the network's condition.
- **Power at a cost:** Living sculk supports crop growth and passive healing, balanced against the effects of prolonged contact.
- **Ancient workstations:** An Ancient Forge offers item-based enchanting, while a mysterious Ancient Altar offers bargains and prayers.
- **A vulnerable Heart:** Weaken the network to expose the Relic Heart, face its audible defence and final growth attempt, and bring the network to an end.
- **Editable campaign content:** Six default roles, nine lore books, nine authored Events and four loot tables provide a starting point for your own world.
- **Local Event audio:** Bundled sound effects include creator and license credits.

## Installation and world preparation

Install matching **Chronicle Core** and **The Quiet Below** on the server and every player's client. Requires **Minecraft Java 1.21.10**, **Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or later**, **Fabric API** and **Java 21 or later**. Simple Voice Chat is recommended for proximity roleplay and is downloaded separately.

**No handcrafted map is bundled.** Prepare your own world using Chronicle's in-game editor, opened with **O** or **/chronicle** with operator permission. Bind the city, Heart, role destinations, chests and any ship or Region changes you want to use.

Default Events and flags appear on a fresh map. Incomplete Events show **!** with setup details until their required map bindings are configured. Map bindings and Event/flag progress stay with the world; reusable content and server settings retain their existing mod/config storage.

## Public beta

The main mechanics and individual systems have automated and native tests. Map-specific balance, discovery placement and group/voice playtesting remain outstanding. Back up your world and configuration before upgrading.
