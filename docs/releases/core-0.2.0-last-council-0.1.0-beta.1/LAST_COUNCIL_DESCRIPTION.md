# The Last Council

A kingdom needs a ruler. Every House has ambitions, and nobody knows who can be trusted.

The Last Council is a multiplayer political roleplay campaign built on **Chronicle Core**. Players represent noble Houses with their own information, secrets and goals, while a separate player takes the role of **Chancellor** to guide Council meetings and voting.

Propose laws, negotiate alliances, conceal your weaknesses and compete for the throne. Council decisions are binding under the game's rules; voluntary agreements between Houses can be honoured or broken. The story develops through the choices players make together.

## At the Council table

- **Houses and private papers:** Public House information, private secrets, House goals and knowledge about other Houses are available through the personal menu, with physical copies on request.
- **Replayable secrets:** Randomized assignments and shared number variables create different political situations. A secret may be known by nobody, one House or several others, without its owner being told.
- **Motions and ballots:** Propose motions at a bound lectern. The Chancellor leads proceedings and opens public or secret votes, with an electorate captured at opening and choices that cannot be changed after submission.
- **Regency elections:** Persistent candidates, successive ballots, elimination and withdrawal support the struggle for the throne.
- **Treaties and House conflicts:** Negotiate public or private agreements, sign exact terms and gather with the Chancellor to sanctify them. Declare conflicts in person, support allies and negotiate peace.
- **Foreign affairs and rebellion:** Bring foreign war and peace before the Council, declare a rebellion or seek formal reinstatement.
- **Unexpected complications:** Configurable Event pools introduce surprises during Council meetings, including for the Chancellor.
- **Optional food poisoning:** Poison food, conceal your involvement and create a race for an antidote as symptoms worsen.
- **Automatic Voice rooms:** With optional Simple Voice Chat support, entering or leaving a configured Region automatically changes the player's room group.

## Installation and world preparation

Install **Chronicle Core 0.2.0 or newer** and **The Last Council** on the server and every player's client. Requires **Minecraft Java 1.21.10**, **Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or later**, **Fabric API** and **Java 21 or later**.

**No prepared map is bundled.** Prepare your own Council chamber, motion and treaty lecterns, voting Region and any guarded areas or Voice rooms using Chronicle's in-game editor, opened with **O** or **/chronicle** with operator permission. Configure Houses, secrets, goals and Events around your setting, then assign participating players and a separate Chancellor.

Simple Voice Chat is optional and downloaded separately from its author. Automatic Voice rooms use isolated groups rather than physical wall or door acoustics. Remote voice requires working Simple Voice Chat networking in addition to the Minecraft connection.

## Public beta

The main mechanics are implemented and have automated and native multi-client regression coverage. Final content, map-specific balance, group pacing and real voice playtesting remain outstanding. Back up your world and configuration before upgrading.
