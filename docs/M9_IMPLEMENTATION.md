## Ownership

Core owns the bounded generic Game setup tool registry, permissions and menu transport. Quiet Below owns indexing, block-state history, frontier traversal, native sculk attribution and ecology state. Existing bounded M4 stages retain their format and behavior.

Source city and Heart use existing Core map assignments, pinned to saved site IDs/revisions. Index only normally loaded chunks, in bounded slices; incomplete indexing cannot enable growth. Adopt existing source-city sculk automatically. Automatic joining outside the initial source requires physical contact with the Heart or connected sculk. Separate explicit region takeover can adopt disconnected patches without activating them. Every grown block records its original state before changing terrain. Source-adopted features have no invented pre-city terrain.

Each adopted record explicitly declares `cleanup=air` and `adopted=true`; this is the proposed later removal baseline, not a claim that air existed before the city. That ending remains M12. Partial loss of owned vein faces records an observation retaining the original baseline. Player/foreign replacements lose ownership; a still-connected neighbor may later regrow there under the ordinary physical rules.

## Physical rules and work bounds

Growth follows reachable air along exposed surfaces. Frontier samples, local target order and backing-face order vary independently within the existing work budget, producing branching patches rather than insertion-ordered straight rows. Sampling uses reproducible seeds from setup, journal revision and the attempt counter; only outcomes are persisted, so future choices need not be identical after restart. Local air-path discovery around converted blocks allows convex corners to be traversed. A separate bounded scan journals missing reachable exposures of existing rooted cells, so older worlds and newly opened surfaces recover without resetting ownership. Discovery follows open air or owned non-waterlogged veins inside a 3x3x3 neighborhood; it cannot tunnel through a thin sealed wall or seed enclosed solid interiors. Eligible native substrate becomes sculk; other solid materials can support veins. No excavation, isolated remote patches, or distinction between authored and natural structures. A closed passage cannot supply a new air frontier on its far side. Pruned cells lose ownership immediately. Placed sculk joins through continuous physical contact with the Heart or connected living sculk, waking detached branches when the cut is bridged. Detached remnants remain tracked for the later ending and lose vitality while their blocks stay in place. Physical reconnection permits gradual recovery, including from zero. There are no catalyst coverage ranges or expansion nodes.

Only ACTIVE, occupied eligible play without a runtime hold advances ecology. Explicit indexing is metadata preparation and can run while paused. Pauses, empty servers and restarts accrue no catch-up. Never force-load chunks. Unloaded cells are unresolved, never evidence of weakness; the first review conservatively suspends expansion while connectivity is unresolved.

Use fixed per-tick scan/connectivity/write budgets and bounded cells/frontiers. Reaching a capacity or persistence fault holds work visibly. Changes use a durable append-only checksummed journal, not whole-network JSON rewrites. A torn, edited, unsupported or invalid journal is preserved and disables this service. Restart reconciliation observes actual blocks; it never resurrects a missing block from an intent record.

Review limits: source region <=16,777,216 blocks / 256 chunks; 256 scan positions plus <=64 adopted cells per preparation tick; 128 reconciliation and graph nodes per play tick. Vitality separately assesses at most 128 owned positions and six loaded-only neighbors per position each play tick. Exposure discovery separately checks <=32 owned cells, <=27 cached loaded-only reads each, and journals <=64 fresh air positions per play tick. There are <=4 journaled world writes per tick, 131,072 owned positions and 524,288 discovered air cells. Stable slot cursors bound five ownership scans without copying the full position set; holes consume work and concurrent additions do not extend a pass. The append log is capped at 64 MiB / 150,000 records / 64 KiB per line. Capacity exhaustion holds growth; automatic compaction is not implemented. On Windows the journal uses a no-delete-sharing handle plus an exclusive lock, creation-time/stat/tail checks; on providers with file keys it also checks file identity. Uncertain/torn writes preserve evidence and disable the service.

Default pace: one growth attempt per 20 occupied ticks, shortened to 10 after a successful bright exposure (>7 light). Slower / Faster / Reset speed save 0.25x, 0.5x, 1x, 2x, 4x, 8x or 16x in the world journal. The menu shows dark/bright intervals, rounded to game ticks and capped at one attempt per tick. A blocked surface can take longer; these are not guaranteed conversions per second. Pauses and speed changes reset cadence, without wall-clock catch-up. Ready rooted count gives Rooted (below 256), Established (256 through 2047) and Extensive (2048 or more) at default tuning, with a 12.5% exit buffer. Pending or unknown vitality assessment holds new advanced features. Every 32 owned cells can offer a spaced native feature: sensor, established catalyst at a multiple of 96, extensive summoning shrieker at a multiple of 128. These are concrete test defaults, not final balance or a Heart-weakness formula. Features need an exposed owned sculk floor and spacing. M9b supplies dormant/fully sealed vitality loss, exposed rooted recovery (bright exposure twice as fast) and per-world loss/recovery/extent controls. No dormant terrain is deleted; see the vitality contract for defaults, checkpointing and fixed scan bounds.

Stone seals the air path but may itself become sculk. If growth already exists beyond a wall, sever its actual living connections as well; closing air does not erase an existing branch. Non-substrate solids still support veins. Foreign replacements drop the old ownership immediately. Existing/placed sculk can then join through actual physical contact. CONNECT records explicitly use an AIR cleanup baseline for such sculk; this does not claim historical terrain beneath it. These contacts are checked only in occupied active play, with at most 33 x 18 neighbour candidates and four shared ownership/world-write intents per tick.

## Native mechanics

The connected ecology is the only growth system in the configured campaign dimension. Catalyst death/XP intake and native spread ticks are suppressed there, including catalysts not yet adopted/cleaned. Player XP is not consumed. Legacy queued catalyst charge is retained but inert. Other dimensions and unconfigured worlds retain vanilla catalyst behavior. Sensors and shriekers use vanilla active detection/warnings/spawning/despawning, with owned inactive responses gated. Grown extensive shriekers set `can_summon=true`.

First activation certifies the complete ownership scan; additive growth extends certified connectivity without silencing existing native features, while real cuts and unknown cells require a bounded rebuild. World-write observation preserves player pruning and queues newly placed sculk for physical joining or optional cleanup, without loading terrain.

## Menu and persistence

`Game setup > Sculk ecology`: **Index source city**, **Enable growth**, **Disable growth**, **Show root**, **Hide root**, **Slower**, **Faster**, **Reset speed**, **Refresh status**. Show root privately outlines the Heart and supplies its coordinates/dimension, without editing or teleporting. Status distinguishes paused/empty play, disconnected branches, unloaded/rebuilding connections and actual faults, and lists connected/detached counts. A temporarily missing/unloaded Heart waits; restoring the expected anchor triggers recertification without poisoning the journal. Index/enable and speed changes require non-active operator editing; speed changes also require a completed source index. Disable is available to operators during play. Status refresh is read-only; statistics do not invalidate controls on every growth step. Finite fallback: `/chronicles setup tool "quiet_below:ecology" index|enable|disable|show_root|hide_root|slower|faster|speed_reset|refresh`.

Data: `<world>/quiet-below/ecology.jsonl`, strict schema-1 hash-chained envelopes with CONFIGURE / INDEX / INDEX_COMPLETE / MODE / GROW / REMOVE / OBSERVE / CONNECT / CLAIM_START / CLAIM_SCAN / CLAIM_DONE / CLEANUP_MODE / PURGE / SPEED / FRONTIER / VITALITY / VITALITY_RULES payloads. Connection/ownership/cleanup records were added in dev.15; SPEED and FRONTIER are additive in dev.16; vitality/checkpoint rules are additive in dev.18. Do not downgrade a journal containing newer records; rollback restores a stopped backup. Existing stores are unchanged. Source/Heart reassignment or revision changes hold the network rather than silently adopting new geometry. There is no destructive reset/reindex button. Restore a verified stopped-world backup for a discarded experiment. A failed world write durably disables growth when the journal remains writable; a crash before terrain persistence is reconciled conservatively without replaying the attempted mutation.

## Ownership maintenance

**Game setup > Sculk takeover region** assigns any saved Core region in the Heart dimension. Assignment changes no terrain or ownership. **Sculk ownership > Take over region** explicitly adopts existing sculk/veins/catalysts/sensors/shriekers in bounded loaded-only slices while play is non-active. It pins the saved ID/revision/bounds, persists progress, preserves existing ownership baselines and does not wake a disconnected patch. **Stop region takeover** retains already adopted cells. Change/select the optional region again for another takeover.

**Delete non-campaign sculk** is a confirmed, optional persistent rule across all normally loaded chunks of the Heart/source dimension, continuing with later-loaded chunks and new stray placements. Other dimensions remain unchanged. Adopt the regions to preserve first. The rule is off by default; cleanup waits while play is inactive/empty/held or a region takeover is unfinished. At most 512 chunk positions plus 16 changed positions and two deletions are handled per tick, sharing the four-intent engine budget. No chunks are force-loaded. The queue is capped at 16,384 loaded chunks; it conservatively rescans after restart. Physical contacts awaiting joining are queued and retained. **Stop sculk cleanup** prevents future deletion without restoring terrain. See [review repair and maintenance contract](M9_ROOT_RECONNECTION.md).
