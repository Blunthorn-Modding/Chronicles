## The proposed draw

Each House keeps its public identity. For a new game each House receives:

- One of its five private entries.
- One general secret.
- **A configurable number of different goals; default 2.** Different Houses
  can receive the same goals. Record this as an in-game scenario setting,
  chosen before papers are issued.

The House entry supplies a bargaining asset, an exploitable weakness or a
dependency with political consequences. Small maintenance problems and good
deeds alone are not enough. The general secret and goals remain independent
of the House's main business.

Other Houses may learn the selected private information. Nobody, one House
or several may know it. Everyone learning the same entry receives the same
facts, and its owner is not told who knows.

Goals describe what a character wants. They do not tell the player how to
play or define a scored success state. Candidate-only goals are reserved for
characters already assigned as candidates.

Check that the configured number of goals is available to each House.
Political tensions can be interesting; goals do not need to come from
different categories. Redraw only goals whose actual demands cannot sensibly
be pursued together.
Do not reject a principled goal just because the character has a shameful past.

The needs question is open again. The [design analysis](NEEDS_AND_GOAL_COUNT.md)
compares optional needs with the current draw and a full resource system.
No needs system has been approved or implemented. Existing private entries
can already contain a pressure, such as a debt or a failed weapons order.

## Variables

Text such as `${number.capacity}` marks a number that would be rolled once
before the entry reaches a player. It is not a range the player must interpret.

For example:

> Draft: Your House can supply `${number.capacity}` other Houses.
>
> After a roll: Your House can supply **3** other Houses.

The author table gives the permitted range, such as **2–5**. Copies of the
entry keep the same number. These are proposed content values; this workbook
does not change the existing draw.

## Editing

You can edit the text directly. References such as **H01-I02**, **S12**, **G08**
and **C05** make it easy to identify an entry in feedback. Author notes and
variable tables are separate from the text intended for players.

The proposed selection work belongs to Last Council. Distinguishing the two
private pools will need later work; no implementation is underway and no
Chronicle Core change is currently proposed.
