# The setting

[Workbook](README.md) · [Houses](HOUSES.md)

## What everyone knows

The king has died without an agreed heir. The noble Houses have gathered at
the royal palace to govern the kingdom and elect a new ruler. Each participating
House has an equal Council vote. The Chancellor chairs the meetings and does
not represent a House.

The Houses rule different parts of the kingdom. Each is known for a particular
trade, resource or source of influence. None controls everything the kingdom
needs. Their public reputations do not reveal their actual reserves, hidden
businesses or private ambitions.

The palace is neutral ground. A House can quarrel with another while remaining
part of the Council. A House that declares rebellion gives up its Council
rights and palace access.

## Neighbouring countries

- **Rheven** is a neighbouring trading country that sells grain, provides
  shipping and lends money.
- **Vask** is a neighbouring kingdom across the land border. Border communities
  trade and have family ties on both sides.

Neither country starts as a friend or enemy. References to them in individual
entries include a short explanation so players do not need to learn this page.

## Notes for the author

- Candidate status is assigned before play. Goals do not offer a separate way
  to become a candidate.
- Council decisions are binding within the story. Agreements between Houses
  are voluntary and can be broken.
- There is no extra law book to learn. An entry involving a crime says plainly
  what happened: bribery, theft, a forged order or another clear act.
- Financial descriptions explain the impact on a House. There is no invented
  currency or requirement to collect money items.
- Each character receives two different goals; other Houses may share them.
  There is no separate needs system.
- House resources and weaknesses matter at the scale of provinces and royal
  policy. Weapons, armies and supplies are bargaining commitments in the story;
  they do not add a troop-management or battle system.
- Home estates and outside events can exist in the story without being built
  on the palace map.
- If a country is removed from the setting, revise entries that depend on it.
  Talvern's public description assumes Vask exists.

This remains a worldbuilding draft. No mod defaults, map or code have changed.
