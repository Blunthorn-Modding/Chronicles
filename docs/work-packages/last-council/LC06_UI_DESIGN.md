# LC06 rebellion and palace access - revised UI proposal

Status: **Revised workflow approved by the owner on 18 November 2026; native implementation and automated verification complete.** [Interactive prototype](../../design/last-council-rebellion.html). Fixture Houses and statements are illustrative. Reviewer controls sit outside the simulated game frame and are not in-game authority controls. Native evidence and unpassed human review gates are recorded in [LC06 results](LC06_RESULTS.md).

## Existing screens and proposed additions

- **My house > Rebellions:** add Rebellions to the existing left-hand section list alongside House information, Secrets, Goals and Other houses' secrets. It shows existing public factions, their founder, statement, members and history, plus **Declare rebellion** and the selected faction's **Join rebellion** action. A public notice opens the same registry content. Existing House information and private sections retain their normal purpose. Narrow layouts keep content bounded and scrollable.
- **Declare or join:** a public statement is required for a new faction. Both paths explain the loss of Council seat, voting, proposing and sponsorship, candidacy and palace access, and withdrawal from an ongoing succession. Reinstatement cannot return a House to a locked or advanced slate. A separate confirmation repeats the consequences. Players remain active participants. There is no free Leave action; ordinary House war has none of these effects.
- **Motion draft:** use the existing motion flow with a typed **Reinstate Houses** category and selection of named rebel Houses. The fixed two-thirds rule applies. Only a successfully closed ballot restores Council rights and palace access; failed or void ballots change nothing. Preserve existing title, proposal text, visibility, sponsorship, admission and public history controls. No alternative no-Council reinstatement workflow is proposed.
- **Regions:** retain the existing Regions list, selected Region and **Choose subtypes** flow. The generic Region access capability is reusable; Last Council supplies the rebel restriction. A compact outside Point field uses the existing Locations picker. A missing, inside, unavailable or unsafe Point refuses declaration and joining before membership changes. No independent palace setup framework or new permission levels are proposed.
- **Chancellor proceedings:** **Allow one palace visit** grants a selected rebel House a supervised palace visit. It ends on exit, and another entry requires a new grant; it restores no Council rights. The owner confirmed retaining this single Chancellor action on 18 November; the revised overall workflow is approved. There is no standalone visit menu or assumed manual revocation. Reviewer-only controls simulate entry and exit.

## Guards and role boundaries

The server must revalidate membership, selected faction, authority, open ballot, crown status, Region binding and safe outside Point at commitment. Membership changes are refused during an open ballot to preserve the captured electorate. A crowned House cannot use the ordinary rebellion action. A completed declaration is public and safely moves an inside representative outside. Access must be enforced across movement, teleports, portals, reconnect, respawn and vehicles/passengers, with uncertain operations recovered without a half-rebel state. Loyal Houses at war remain eligible. Editing/Building access exceptions do not grant political rights. The Chancellor is a separate role with no assumed House; Region editing remains explicitly operator-only. No automatic combat, armies, resources, forced settlement, map damage or winner is introduced.
