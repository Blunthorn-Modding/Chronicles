# LC06 rebellion and palace access — UI draft

Status: **design draft; owner policies confirmed; proposed workflow review pending**. [Interactive scene prototype](../../design/last-council-rebellion.html). Illustrative Houses and statements are fixture content, not campaign canon. The reviewer controls are outside the simulated game frame; they are not in-game authority controls. No native implementation or verification is claimed.

## Scenes and entry points

- **My house → House information:** retain the current Character / My house / Other houses tabs, existing section list and paper workflow. Add a compact Council participation status and **Declare rebellion…** plus **View rebellions**. Do not reproduce private Secrets, Goals or other Houses' secrets in this action. The declaration is a focused public-statement form followed by a separate confirmation. It explicitly says the House renounces Council/Crown authority and loses its seat, voting, motion proposal and sponsorship, candidacy and palace access. Rebellion withdraws the House from an ongoing succession; reinstatement cannot return it to an already locked or advanced slate. Configured candidacy before a succession begins remains a separate later policy. The player remains an active participant. An ordinary house war does not entail these consequences.
- **Public notice or My house → Rebellions:** use a standalone compact list-left/detail-right registry like Conflicts. The list represents distinct factions; the selected record contains its founder, public statement, member Houses and public history. **Join rebellion…** identifies that exact faction and repeats the same consequences before confirmation. A separate loyal House may declare its own faction. There is no free Leave action. At narrow widths, stack the list over the detail, with each region bounded and scrollable.
- **Motion lectern / Chancellor proceedings → motion draft:** add the typed **Reinstate Houses** category to the existing flow. Select named rebel Houses and display the fixed two-thirds rule. Only a successfully closed ballot reinstates the selected Houses and restores their Council participation and palace access. Reinstatement does not return anyone to an already locked or advanced succession slate. Failed and void votes do not change membership. Use the normal proposal, sponsorship, admission, ballot and public history routes rather than adding a direct restore button.
- **Regions / Locations → Palace Region:** extend the existing Region subtype UI with **Guarded access** and an outside Point picker drawing from existing Locations. Show the selected Point and preflight status. A missing, inside, unavailable or unsafe Point refuses declaration/joining before any membership or access change. Do not create a second Region or Locations catalogue. Core owns reusable server-authoritative movement protection; Last Council supplies palace eligibility and political operations.
- **Explicit supervised visit:** the Chancellor grants a named rebellious House one supervised visit. Show awaiting entry, inside, and ended on exit. Leaving the palace ends that visit; a new entry requires a new grant. The exception never restores a Council seat or vote. Manual revocation is not specified. Editing/Building Mode access exceptions are authoring access only and never grant a political seat or candidacy.

## Checks and consequences

The server must revalidate membership and target faction, authority, the open-ballot guard, crown status, Region binding and outside Point at commitment, not merely when showing a button. Membership changes are refused during any open ballot so its captured electorate is unchanged. The crowned House cannot use the ordinary rebellion action; a later crown crisis has separate rules. A completed declaration is public, removes Council privileges and safely moves an inside representative outside; the player remains a participant. Ordinary entry must be guarded across movement, teleports, portals, reconnect, respawn and vehicles/passengers. Fail closed on stale or unsafe state without leaving a half-rebel; preserve and recover uncertain operations. Loyal Houses at war remain eligible for the palace and Council. No automatic combat, armies, resources, forced settlement, map damage or winner is introduced.

The prototype's disabled buttons communicate the refusal, and the confirmation repeats the same checks to illustrate revalidation. Its sample mutation merely demonstrates the visual transition; it does not establish a persistence or access mechanism.

## Confirmed policies and proposed workflow

The owner confirmed one supervised visit ends when the rebel leaves the palace. The illustrated grant and state presentation are proposed for review; no general revocation policy is assumed.

The owner confirmed **reinstatement without a Council** requires each affected House's consent and a public Chancellor confirmation. It is available only when no eligible Council House exists at all, not merely when eligible representatives are absent from the voting Region. The proposed Chancellor-menu flow names the affected Houses and public reason or terms, displays each House's consent to the exact same version, and enables public confirmation only after all affected Houses consent. Changes to terms or parties invalidate prior consents. Do not force signatures or accept an empty public reason. Presence and delivery mechanics must support the affected participants, but this proposal does not require a treaty lectern or invent an attendance ceremony. Normal reinstatement uses the typed two-thirds motion whenever an eligible Council exists.

The sample reviewer panel simulates the no-Council condition and visit entry/exit; it is outside the game frame. Proposed control placement and the consent workflow still require owner review.
