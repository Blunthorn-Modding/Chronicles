# LC-08 — Content, complications and existing Event integration

Status: planned. Depends on LC-01; integrate each delivered operation from LC-02–07. Brief: §§3, 10–13, 15–16.

## Outcome and ownership

Use existing Roles, Books & Lore, loot, Events and flags to author the evening. Last Council adds political-operation source providers and its own starting content; Core remains the reusable editor/executor. Flags are saved state, not another Event/action engine.

## Work

1. Review the illustrative cast before making it playable defaults. Every selected role needs leverage, a private ambition/alternative and reasons to approach at least two others. Keep objectives narrative; no automatic scoreboard.
2. Author public/private letters and treaties through existing book/delivery facilities. Keep secrets out of ordinary role/public projections; do not build another dossier/lore catalogue.
3. Prepare optional host-triggered complications: testament, ultimatum, chalice, treasury or royal seal. Use existing text/sound/lore and, where necessary, typed agenda actions with explicit review; no hard session schedule or forced response.
4. Register source receipts for completed votes, treaty actions, conflict changes, rebellion/reinstatement and succession results. Events can update flags/read prerequisites or deliver narrative cues after those operations commit. Preview/retry/reconnect must not mint new political occurrences.
5. Reuse scenario export/import for portable content and deliberate extensions for political rules. Exclude electorate choices, signatures, discoveries and campaign progress. Map Regions/Points stay with the map.

## Acceptance and verification

Fresh-map setup shows intended defaults and clear `!` diagnostics for missing bindings. Prepared-world and custom-profile round trips preserve appropriate definitions/progress without importing Quiet Below defaults. Private complications reach only their intended recipients; a timer reminder does not automatically convene a Council.

Exactly-once sources across interruption/restart, preview without side effects, flags updated through Events and no secret-vote data in source/history payloads. A crisis cannot force a signature, change a closed ballot or add a candidate to a locked slate without a transparent reset.

## Review gate

Content/role/lore and recipient review comes after the supporting mechanics are usable. The brief's examples remain examples until approved; no invented rightful heir or preassigned ending.
