## Ownership and player rule

Quiet Below owns contact detection, local-cost state, native effect ownership, saved tuning and diagnostics. Core remains unchanged: it supplies the existing participant/author/ACTIVE eligibility and generic Game setup UI only. There are no hazard regions, narrative unlocks, new entities, block entities, assets, registries or protocol frames.

An eligible Survival or Adventure participant must be physically standing on an owned, rooted, living campaign sculk block or supported downward sculk vein at 25%+ vitality. The native contact test is loaded-only and checks actual collision surfaces across the complete sole footprint. Any intersecting qualifying support retains contact at a seam; a wholly uninfested landing clears it. A floor vein must coat an actual supporting collision surface. Side-only nearby veins do not infest the surface underfoot or defeat a clean pillar. The operator confirms jumping retains exposure until a non-sculk landing. Walking between qualifying living blocks and airborne movement retain exposure from the last living footing; airborne players cannot start exposure without prior living footing, and that source is revalidated while airborne. An early grounded movement packet with no collision reaching the sole remains airborne even when its provisional support position changes. Standing entirely on other blocks, disconnection, inactive/weak/unresolved roots, pause/hold, loss of participation, spectator/creative/author state, death, dimension change or logout removes contact immediately, clears both pending waits and withdraws only the campaign's own active effects.

Hunger is available in Established or Extensive growth; Darkness additionally requires Extensive growth. Each begins after its own fresh initial wait, defaulting to 40 eligible seconds; no repeated pulse cadence follows. Once applied, the effect stays active only while valid physical contact remains. Controls for each rule are independent: enabled/disabled and 10, 20, 40, 60 or 120 initial seconds, default enabled/40 seconds. Changing a rule clears derived contact state and any effect owned by that rule.

The implementation must not make a player carry campaign costs after stepping off the ecology. It also must not erase or rewrite a foreign potion/Warden effect. Campaign effects are marked by their originating effect instance, filtered from player save snapshots, and removed only while the same tracked instance remains present. Native effect merging removes the marked campaign instance before merging ordinary effects, preserving foreign effects and vanilla hidden stacks. A direct instance-upgrade fallback may retain a one-tick campaign marker so a foreign effect can be promoted. Arbitrary third-party mutations of raw internal effect maps remain outside the supported integration contract.

Campaign-owned effects use native infinite duration while contact remains valid; the server withdraws them on exit and filters them from player saves. There is no periodic renewal after the first wait.

## Bounded work and persistence

The service keeps at most 256 contact states. Above 256 online players it holds before enumerating participants or reading contact blocks. A direct contact proof reads at most twelve cached cells (a2x2 footprint, two support heights and the possible floor coating). The early-grounded exception additionally examines at most eight cached cells (the same footprint at two heights), plus revalidation of the one saved living source. Larger footprints or missing evidence fail closed; at most256 initial proofs and1,024 per-kind cleanup/application guards run per tick. Per-kind eligibility guards use current constant-time participation/author/phase checks, rather than enumerating all players again. Grounded collision-height tolerance is1/32 block, below carpet height. An early grounded packet retains the previous living source while no collision touches the complete sole footprint, irrespective of the provisional stepping position. A newly placed pillar/carpet blocks that exception. All collision-shape queries use an empty block view and cannot obtain world chunk tickets. The service does not search for nearby sculk, create tickets, add growth/terrain writes or change growth/crop/healing budgets. Invalid/missing loaded evidence withdraws campaign effects.

`COST_RULES` remains the strict persisted record for Hunger and Darkness. `SPREAD_RULES` persists the separate growth-mode selection below. Checkpoint format 4 contains both rules; formats 1, 2 and 3 restore the fixed growth mode and its default amplification of 10. New records require the matching reader. Rollback restores a stopped-world backup and its jars together.

## Growth-mode tuning

Sculk ecology retains its existing base spread speed control: 25, 50, 100, 200, 400, 800 or 1600 percent. A separate **Spread mode** tool chooses:

- **Fixed rate** — the saved base percent is used unchanged. This is the default.
- **Sculk-count rate** — the effective percent is `base * (1 + count * amplification / 100000)`, capped at 1600 percent.

Amplification choices are 0, 1, 2, 5, 10, 25, 50 or 100, shown as `+X% per 1,000 sculk`; 10 is the default. The operator confirms that only living, Heart-connected sculk counts. The last bounded summary of rooted25%+ cells supplies that count; dormant branches do not accelerate connected roots. Fixed rate remains the default and preserves the old base-speed behavior. A mode/rule edit resets the growth cadence; it does not add a scan, mutation allowance or bypass existing indexed/eligible-play requirements.
