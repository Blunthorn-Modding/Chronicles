# Written clues: The Quiet Below

Use the text exactly or change names and local details to fit the players' established story. Keep a public board copy and a second environmental copy of each. A player who hears a clue read aloud counts as having access to it; no item, skill, or GM permission is required.

## 1. Surveyor's margin — “The line that was not there”

**Board copy:** signed notice titled *Surveyor's Margin*.  
**Environmental copy:** loose notebook on the Marker alcove shelf at absolute `(1, 57, -37)`.

> The north wall has acquired a line where yesterday there was only stone. It is too clean for a pick and too recent for a seam. When I put my palm against it, the vibration was not under the rock. It was coming through it, as though the valley had answered from the other side.
>
> I have marked the place and told no one which explanation I prefer. A crack can be a warning. It can also be a door.

**What it gives:** a recent, unexplained change beneath the mine; an invitation to investigate, protect, close off, or question the premise. The Marker observation and any mine visitor can independently point players here.

## 2. Work-order reversal — “Keep the supports”

**Board copy:** workshop notice headed *Work Order, amended*.  
**Environmental copy:** folded sheet in the unlocked workshop barrel at absolute `(24, 66, 1)`.

> Stop cutting the old supports until someone has looked at the chamber. The timber is not failing from rot. It is bearing a weight that is moving in slow, patient intervals.
>
> If the mine is unsafe, we can shore it and leave it. If it is asking to be opened, we should decide together what we owe the people sleeping above it. Bring your reasons, not just your tools.

**What it gives:** a practical stake for builders and protectors, plus a social question about consent and risk. Players can reach the same issue by seeing the supports, discussing the mine, or using the Lens on the Marker during an active session.

## 3. Unsent letter — “A valley remembers”

**Board copy:** copied letter without a recipient.  
**Environmental copy:** sealed-but-unlocked letter on the west-passage lectern at absolute `(-12, 55, -36)`.

> I went down expecting a vein of ore and found a place that made room for my footsteps. The sound changed when I spoke kindly, and changed again when I spoke of taking everything we could carry.
>
> I cannot say whether that means there is a mind below us. I can say the valley remembers pressure. Whatever we choose, let it be a choice we can repeat in daylight.

**What it gives:** evidence for several interpretations without confirming one: a living presence, a geological reaction, a warning, or a reflection of the settlement's conduct. It opens containment, study, departure, care, extraction with safeguards, or a player-invented response.

## Reuse rules

- Reveal a clue as a readable object, a spoken recollection, or a copied notice; never as an NPC quest reward.
- Do not hide all copies behind the future M4 survey seam, a combat threat, or a single character's background.
- When a clue is discovered, ask the finder what their character makes of it before adding more information.
- If a later session needs a new angle, annotate a copy with a player decision rather than replacing the original text. This lets the world respond without declaring a predetermined answer.
