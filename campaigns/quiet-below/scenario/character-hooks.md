# Public character-hook prompts

Players may use none, one, or several of these in their public dossier description. They are conversation starters, not jobs, mechanics, secrets, or an instruction to take a side.

| Public hook | A question the player can answer in play |
| --- | --- |
| Mine worker or survey helper | What makes a familiar passage feel wrong to you? |
| Builder, carpenter, or mason | What would you refuse to sacrifice to keep the settlement safe? |
| Trader, prospector, or quartermaster | When does a useful discovery become too costly? |
| Gardener, healer, or caretaker | Who is most affected if the valley changes slowly rather than violently? |
| Local resident | What ordinary place here do you want to preserve? |
| New arrival | What promise or rumor brought you to a settlement with a closed mine? |
| Amateur historian or collector | What old story do you suspect has been simplified too much? |
| Craftsperson | What practical tool or repair would you offer before you offer an opinion? |
| Watcher or worried neighbor | Who do you want to hear before the community makes a risky decision? |
| Visitor with no stake yet | What would make this place feel like home—or make you leave? |

Ask each player for a public name, optional pronouns, and a short description for the existing dossier. Private motives stay with the player; no player must reveal one to participate. At Session 0, invite every character to choose one place in the site they care about and one other character they would ask for advice.
